# Trip reports

A rider or driver reports the other person on a trip (misconduct, safety, disputes); staff triage; the reporter hears the outcome. The person reported is **never told** (no socket event, no email) so a report can't invite retaliation. SOS (`sos` module) is the alarm for an emergency in progress; a report is the record.

- **Permission module `reports`**, separate from `users` and `sos`: `GET /admin/reports*` need `reports: read`, `PATCH /admin/reports/:id` needs `reports: update`. Filing, listing, reading and withdrawing your own reports need no permission, and ownership is the filter: every own-report query is `where reporterUserId = caller`, so anyone else (the person reported, strangers) gets 404, never 403. The router is registered **before `adminUserRouter`**, whose `/admin/:userId` would swallow `/admin/reports`.
- **Who may file** (`createReport`): the trip's rider or driver; the reported person is derived from the trip (the other one), never sent. Admins can't (403). A trip must have been accepted: `accepted`/`boarded` always; `completed`/`no_show`/`cancelled` (the last only with `acceptedAt` set) until `completedAt ?? cancelledAt` + the `reports.filingWindowHours` setting (default 72, in `TRIP_SETTINGS`); `pending`/`declined`/`expired` answer 409. `tripModel.settleStale` runs first so a stale trip is seen as what it has become. `tripStatus` snapshots the trip's status at filing ("boarded" = made during the ride).
- **Categories and severity:** `REPORT_CATEGORIES` in `report.schema.ts`; `URGENT_REPORT_CATEGORIES` (assault, threats, sexual misconduct, unsafe driving, suspected intoxication) make `severity = "urgent"`. The client never sends severity (extra body fields are stripped). Urgent reports sort first in the staff queue and their confirmation email points to 112/SOS.
- **One live report per trip + reporter + category:** the partial unique index `tripReports_one_live_per_category` (status open/underReview) decides, so two simultaneous identical filings give 201 + 409. Closing or withdrawing frees the category again.
- **Status machine** (`open → underReview → resolved | dismissed`, reporter may `withdrawn`): every transition is one guarded `UPDATE ... WHERE status IN ('open','underReview')` (`moveStatusByAdmin`, `withdraw` in `report.model.ts`), so a withdraw racing a staff close has exactly one winner; the loser gets 409 from `refusal()`. resolved/dismissed/withdrawn are final. `internalNotes` (staff only) and `outcomeMessage` (told to the reporter) use `coalesce` so they only change when given.
- **Evidence goes in the filing request, never a separate upload:** `uploadImages("evidence", 5)` (`src/middlewares/upload.ts`) parses multipart before `validate()`: images only (JPEG/PNG/WEBP), 5MB each, max 5; a JSON request passes through with no files (base64-in-JSON is deliberately unsupported: `express.json()` caps bodies at 100kb). The controller generates the report id first, uploads to `reports/<id>` through `storage.service`, then inserts; if an upload or the insert fails, the files already uploaded are `deleteFile`d. `storageKey` stays in the `evidence` jsonb and is stripped from every view (`evidenceUrls`).
- **Views:** reporter view = `reporterView` (no staff notes, no `handledByAdminId`, no user ids); staff view adds them plus, on `GET /admin/reports/:id`, both people with phones, the trip, and `history` counts (reports filed by the reporter, reports against the reported person and how many are unresolved). Queue order: unresolved first, urgent first, newest first.
- **Privacy:** the audit trail never holds what was said: `logActivity` gets `auditView` (id, category, severity, status, evidence count) with `redact: ["description"]` / `["internalNotes", "outcomeMessage"]`, and `httpLogger` blanks `description` for `POST /trips/:id/reports`. Staff reading a report or the queue is audited (`report.view`, `report.list`).
- **Notifications:** `report:created` → room `admin:reports` (admins with `reports: read` join in `joinSocketRooms`); `report:statusChanged` → the reporter's `user:` room (staff moves only) and the desk (staff moves and withdrawals). Email goes to the reporter only (`sendReportEmail`). Rate limit `reportLimit` (20 per 15 min per account) sits before the upload so it also caps image traffic.
- **Tests** (`tests/reports.test.ts`): reports staff act on are written straight to the DB with `seedReport` because creating through the API is rate limited per account (a test that files through the API uses the driver, or a fresh reported rider, to stay under 20 and to keep email assertions about "the reported person" unambiguous).
